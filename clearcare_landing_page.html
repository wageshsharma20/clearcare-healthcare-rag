FEATURE 01 • ANTI-VECTOR BLUR

Targeted Biomarker Scanner: Every lab test gets individual attention.

Blood work often lists 14 or more different values on a single page. Generic search tools blur all these numbers together into one generic embedding. ClearCare AI scans each biomarker individually—such as Glucose, Creatinine, or Platelets—and retrieves the exact, dedicated guideline chunk for each one.

  • Eliminates context dilution across multi-analyte blood panels.
  • 120-character sliding window overlap preserves qualification clauses.
FEATURE 02 • DENSE SEMANTIC EMBEDDINGS

384-Dimensional Neural Matching: Understands what you mean, not just keywords.

If your hospital discharge paperwork says "hyperglycemia" and you ask about "high blood sugar", old-fashioned keyword search fails completely. ClearCare AI uses `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` to map medical words into a 384-dimensional mathematical space where synonyms naturally align, ensuring accurate retrieval regardless of patient phrasing.

  • Runs 100% locally on CPU using ChromaDB ($0 recurring vector database fees).
  • HNSW multi-layer graph indexing searches chunks in under 5 milliseconds.
