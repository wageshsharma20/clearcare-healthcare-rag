                Step 01 • Trusted Medical Rules
                5 Official Guidelines
              

              

                📚 Trusted Medical Guidelines (No Random Internet Guessing)
              

              

                Regular chatbots search the open web, which often finds scary stories or false advice. ClearCare does not guess. 
                It only uses real medical rules written by verified doctors and hospitals: the American Heart Association (AHA), 
                American College of Cardiology (ACC), American Association for Clinical Chemistry (AACC), 
                and NIH MedlinePlus. If a rule is not proven by doctors, ClearCare never touches it.
              

              

                

                  1. Blood Chemistry (CMP)
                  Kidney health (BUN, Creatinine, eGFR), Liver enzymes (ALT, AST), and Blood Sugar.
                

                

                  2. Complete Blood Count (CBC)
                  Red cells (anemia check), White cells (infection defense), and Platelets (blood clotting).
                

                

                  3. Cholesterol & Heart Panel
                  Total cholesterol, protective HDL ("good"), bad LDL targets (<100 or <70), and Triglycerides.
                

                

                  4. Thyroid Energy Panel
                  TSH and T4 hormones that manage your daily energy, weight, and metabolism.
                

                

                  5. Heart Stent Discharge Guide
                  Why taking blood-thinning pills (Aspirin + Plavix) every day is mandatory, plus what symptoms require immediate emergency care.
                

              

            


            
            

              

                Step 02 • Smart Cutting
                700 characters + Heading Tag
              

              

                ✂️ Smart Topic Cutting (Never Losing the Context)
              

              

                Medical guidelines are long. If you cut a paragraph in half, you might separate an important warning (like "normal after exercise") 
                from the lab number itself. ClearCare cuts guidelines into clean, bite-sized pieces. It stamps the document title and topic heading right at the top of every piece so the AI always knows what test it is reading.
              

              

                
# Clear Topic Label Attached to Every Chunk:

                
# Document: metabolic_panel_cmp.md > Topic: Kidney Function & Filtration

                
Serum Creatinine reference interval: 0.6 - 1.3 mg/dL. Higher numbers can mean kidney stress, but temporary rises often happen from mild dehydration, heavy workouts, or high-protein meals...

              

            


            
            

              

                Step 03 • Word Meaning
                384 Meaning Numbers • 100% Private
              

              

                🧠 Understanding Medical Meaning (Not Just Keywords)
              

              

                Patients and doctors often use different words for the exact same condition. A patient might say "high blood sugar", while a doctor writes "hyperglycemia". 
                A simple search bar would miss the connection! ClearCare uses a smart language model that turns every sentence into 384 numbers representing its exact medical meaning. 
                This lets ClearCare match synonyms easily. It runs on your computer, so your private medical records never leave your device.
              

            


            
            

              

                Step 04 • Fast Search
                ChromaDB Local Library
              

              

                🗄️ Instant Guideline Search (ChromaDB)
              

              

                All guideline pieces are saved inside a fast local database called ChromaDB. When you ask a question or paste a report, ChromaDB uses a smart index (called an HNSW graph) 
                to flip directly to the exact right medical guideline in less than one second.
              

            


            
            

              

                Step 05 • Thorough Checking
                Checking Every Test
              

              

                🔎 Checking Every Test Individually (No Missing Numbers)
              

              

                Why normal AI fails on blood tests: If you upload a lab report with 15 numbers, regular AI tries to read everything all at once. The numbers get blurred together, and it often forgets to check specific tests like your ALT or LDL.
              

              

                

                   What Regular AI Does (Blurred Search)
                  

                    Reads 15 blood tests as one big paragraph → gets confused → gives general advice → misses an elevated liver or kidney warning.
                  

                

                

                   What ClearCare Does (Test-by-Test)
                  

                    Checks every test name one by one (Glucose, ALT, eGFR, Potassium) → finds the exact rule for each → combines everything cleanly. Nothing gets missed.
                  

                

              

            


            
            

              

                Step 06 • Relevance Shield
                Drops Weak Matches (< 25%)
              

              

                🎯 The Relevance Shield (Throwing Out Wrong Clues)
              

              

                In healthcare, reading the wrong guideline is dangerous because it leads to bad advice. ClearCare has a strict rule: if a guideline matches by less than 25%, it is immediately thrown out. 
                Only strong, verified medical guidelines are allowed into the final answer.
              

            


            
            

              

                Step 07 • Careful AI Brain
                Llama 3.3 / Groq • Strict Safety Locks
              

              

                ⚡ Safe, Guardrailed AI Brain (Llama 3.3 on Groq)
              

              

                Our AI brain reads your question alongside the verified hospital rules we found. We set 4 unbreakable safety locks:
              

              

                

                  1. No Guessing Diseases
                  It will never tell you "You have a disease". It explains what the number means and advises talking with your doctor.
                

                

                  2. Simple, Harmless Causes First
                  It always explains harmless causes first (like dehydration, exercise, or diet) so you don't panic needlessly.
                

                

                  3. Clear Guideline Source Tags
                  Every statement is tagged with [Source X] so you can see the real medical guideline behind it.
                

                

                  4. Medication Safety Lock
                  It is strictly locked from ever telling a patient to stop taking prescribed medicines like Plavix or blood thinners.
