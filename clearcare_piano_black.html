Feature 01

Targeted multi-query biomarker expansion.

When you give standard RAG a lab report with 15 tests, it merges everything into one centroid average vector. This is called Vector Blur, and it routinely misses isolated liver elevations. ClearCare identifies every individual analyte via clinical regex and performs targeted sub-queries for each test before deduplicating results.

  • Zero dilution across multi-analyte panels
  • Independent matching against 5 clinical guidelines
Feature 02

Local 384-dimensional dense semantic vectors.

Medical vocabulary is notoriously varied: a patient says "sugar spike" while a discharge note states "hyperglycemia". Our distilled MiniLM transformer maps concepts into a 384-dimensional geometric space where synonymous medical concepts reside close together. Because it runs locally on CPU, zero Protected Health Information (PHI) leaves your device.

  • ChromaDB HNSW logarithmic graph indexing (O(log N))
  • Cosine thresholding (≥ 0.25) to discard irrelevant noise
