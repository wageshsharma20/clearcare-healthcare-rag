🎓 Core RAG Concepts for Viva & Technical Evaluations


          

            
1. Why RAG in Healthcare?

            
Standard LLMs hallucinate medical dosages and normal ranges. RAG decouples non-parametric memory (verified guidelines) from parametric generation, guaranteeing traceability and 100% auditability.

          


          

            
2. Chunking & Overlap Mechanics

            
We use 700-character chunks with 120-character overlap. 700 chars represents an individual clinical assay without vector dilution. 120 chars prevents boundary truncation of medical warnings.

          


          

            
3. Vector Embeddings & Cosine Distance

            
Uses all-MiniLM-L6-v2 to map text into 384 dimensions. Cosine distance d = 1 - cos(θ) measures directional divergence between query and guideline vectors.

          


          

            
4. Vector Blur & Multi-Query Resolution

            
Multi-analyte lab panels cause vector averaging blur. ClearCare runs regex entity scanning across 30+ biomarkers, firing targeted sub-queries and merging candidate chunks.

          


          

            
5. Medical Safety Guardrails

            
Low temperature (0.2), absolute diagnosis prohibition, mandatory [Source X] citations, benign cause explanations, and doctor question checklists.
